<h1><span class="l-en">Join us</span><span class="l-zh">加入我们</span></h1>

<p class="lede l-en">We take undergraduates, master's students and doctoral students. What is required is quantum mechanics at the level of a solid undergraduate course and a willingness to program. Prior nuclear physics is not required and, for a beginning student, is not even an advantage.</p>
<p class="lede l-zh">我们招本科生、硕士和博士。要求是扎实的本科量子力学，以及愿意写程序。不要求核物理背景，对刚起步的学生来说，有没有这个背景甚至算不上优势。</p>

<h2><span class="l-en">Undergraduates</span><span class="l-zh">本科生</span></h2>

<p class="l-en">A first project here does not begin with review articles. It begins with a calculation you run yourself: take a global optical potential, compute the elastic angular distribution for a real system, pull the measured points out of the database, and put both on the same plot. Within an afternoon the diffraction minima either line up or they do not, and you have learned something about the world by computing it. Very few fields can hand a second-year student that experience, and it is the single strongest argument for doing nuclear theory rather than reading about physics.</p>
<p class="l-zh">在这里，第一个课题不是从读综述开始的，而是从一个你自己跑起来的计算开始：拿一个全局光学势，算出某个真实体系的弹性散射角分布，再从数据库里把实验点取出来，画在同一张图上。一个下午之内，衍射极小要么对上，要么没对上，而你是通过亲手计算了解了关于这个世界的一件事。能让大二学生得到这种体验的领域不多，这也是选择做核理论、而不是只读物理的最有力的理由。</p>

<p class="l-en">Suitable entry points include a scattering calculation compared against measured data, a reproduction of a published cross section to a stated number of digits, or a small piece of one of the group codes. Undergraduate research projects, thesis projects and summer work are all possible. Write with a short note about which courses you have taken and what you would like to try.</p>
<p class="l-zh">合适的入门题目包括：一个与实验数据对照的散射计算；把一篇已发表论文的截面复现到给定的有效位数；或者组里某个代码中的一个小模块。大创、毕业设计和暑期科研都可以。来信时写清楚修过哪些课，以及你想试试什么方向。</p>

<h2><span class="l-en">Master's and doctoral students</span><span class="l-zh">硕士与博士研究生</span></h2>

<p class="l-en">Admission runs through the Department of Physics at Tongji University on the normal annual cycle. Students in the group take on a research line and a code together: the two are not separable here, and by graduation you should own a piece of software that other people use. Recent theses have been built around inclusive breakup with microscopic projectile structure, surrogate reactions, and few-body approaches to nuclear decay.</p>
<p class="l-zh">招生通过同济大学物理科学与工程学院按每年的常规流程进行。进组的学生同时接手一条研究线和一套代码，这两者在这里是分不开的；到毕业时，你应该拥有一件别人也在用的软件。近几年的学位论文分别围绕带微观入射核结构的非弹性破裂、替代反应，以及用少体方法处理核衰变展开。</p>

<p class="l-en">If you are considering applying, contact us before the application deadline rather than after. A short exchange about what you want to work on is worth more than a formal letter.</p>
<p class="l-zh">如果你在考虑申请，请在截止日期之前而不是之后联系我们。就你想做什么先聊几句，比一封正式的信更有用。</p>
